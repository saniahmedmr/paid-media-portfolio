Crisper Kit

Meal-kit subscription brand running Meta (Facebook & Instagram) campaigns. Full-funnel management: cold traffic prospecting + retargeting. Campaign objective: customer acquisition optimized for subscription LTV.

Why CPP alone does not tell the full story here: Crisper Kit is a subscription business. A customer acquired at $37 does not generate $37 in revenue — they generate it every month for as long as they stay subscribed. Even the cold traffic $89 CPP is profitable if the average subscriber stays active for 3+ months. The retargeting $9.38 CPP is an exceptionally strong return.

Campaign Performance Analysis

Cost-Per-Purchase: Retargeting vs. Cold Traffic

Key insight: Retargeting converts customers at $9.38 — 9.5× cheaper than cold traffic ($89.17). This validates a clear full-funnel structure: cold traffic builds the audience; retargeting converts efficiently. For a subscription business, the $9.38 CPP means the campaign pays back in under a month.

Budget Allocation vs. Customers Acquired

Key insight: Cold traffic consumed 84% of spend but generated only 35% of purchases. Retargeting used 16% of budget and delivered 65% of purchases. This is the expected funnel dynamic — and signals clear room to scale retargeting budget for improved overall ROAS.

Spend vs. Revenue by Campaign

Key insight: Retargeting generated $111,906 in attributed revenue on $13,732 in spend — an 8.1x return. Cold traffic drove $54,250 on $71,250 spend (0.76x reported ROAS), but this reflects early-subscriber LTV lag; subscribers acquired from cold traffic are still generating monthly recurring revenue.

Funnel Efficiency Comparison

Key insight: Over 5.1 million impressions delivered with an average CPM of $16.53. Cold traffic serves as a brand-awareness and audience-building layer, while retargeting closes the purchase. The funnel is working as designed for a subscription acquisition model.
