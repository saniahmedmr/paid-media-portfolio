Campaign Performance Charts

Leads Campaign — Key Metrics

1,649 leads from CA$1,335 spend = CA$0.81 CPL. The campaign ran on a multi-attribution setting targeting warm ALS-adjacent audiences. CTR of 2.74% (all clicks) indicates strong creative resonance with the target audience.

Campaign Portfolio — Spend Breakdown

Leads campaign dominated the budget: CA$1,335 (63%) of total account spend went to the leads campaign which delivered all 1,649 leads. Event promotion campaigns (Viva Las Stevie) ran separate objectives targeting ticket sales.

Reach vs. Frequency vs. CPL

Efficient audience utilization: 137,316 unique people reached at 2.68 frequency — meaning most users saw the ad 2–3 times before converting to a lead. This is the ideal frequency range for non-profit lead gen.

CPL vs. Industry Benchmark (CA$)

12× below non-profit lead gen benchmark: At CA$0.81, this campaign delivered leads at roughly 1/12th the cost of typical non-profit Meta campaigns. This reflects exceptional creative-audience fit and the strong organic affinity for ALS causes.
