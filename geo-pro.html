Performance Charts

ROAS by Campaign (%)

Wide ROAS variance across campaigns (15% to 228%) shows the account has been running on autopilot without regular bid and allocation optimization. Shifting budget from poor performers to the 228% ROAS campaign alone would dramatically improve blended results.

Spend Allocation vs. ROAS Contribution

Budget misalignment is the core problem: The best campaign ($20K) is getting 16% of total budget while generating 228% ROAS. The worst campaign ($2.3K, 15% ROAS) should be immediately paused and its budget redirected.

Cost Per Customer by Campaign ($)

Best CAC: $107.67 (Plus & Advanced campaign). For a Shopify app with monthly recurring subscriptions, acquiring a merchant at $108 is highly profitable — typical Shopify app LTV for a geolocation tool runs $200–500+ over the subscription lifetime.

Optimized vs. Current ROAS Projection

Projected post-optimization: Pausing Trial & Retail (15% ROAS), restructuring Basic targeting, and scaling Plus & Advanced to $300/day would realistically improve blended ROAS from 101% to 150%+ on the same monthly budget.

Optimization Strategy Delivered

Key Recommendations from Audit Report (Prepared by Sani Ahmed, May 2026)
✅
Scale Plus & Advanced to $300/day: 228% ROAS at $100/day is severely under-invested. The US (312% ROAS), Canada (475%), UAE (413%) markets all have headroom before saturation.
🚫
Pause Trial & Retail immediately: $2,258 spent, only 6 customers, 15% ROAS. Every dollar here is better placed in the Plus & Advanced campaign.
🔧
Segment Shopify & Basic by plan type: Grow plan users show 188% ROAS; Basic plan users show 38%. Create separate ad groups with separate bids to stop the Basic plan holders from diluting the overall campaign performance.
🧹
Negate 554 zero-revenue search terms: The discovery campaign found 554 keywords that received clicks but generated zero revenue. Negating these recovers $3–5K in annual waste.
📍
Optimize 'geolocation' bid by country: This single keyword accounts for ~$62K+ in spend across all campaigns. Country-level bid adjustments (reduce India by 40%, increase Canada/UAE) would meaningfully improve overall CPA.
